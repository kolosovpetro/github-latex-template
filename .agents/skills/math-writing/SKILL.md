# Mathematical writing

Use this skill when creating or editing the manuscript, its mathematical exposition, or related LaTeX material in this repository.

Before writing or reviewing, read the repository-root `MATHWRITING.md` and apply its rules to the requested material. Treat it as a project style guide, not as a request to rewrite unrelated files. Preserve the mathematical meaning, notation, labels, and references unless the user asks to change them.

When reviewing existing text, report or fix concrete violations in the requested scope. For LaTeX, check the rendered output when available for page-margin overflow, equation breaks, and caption placement; source inspection alone cannot confirm page layout. Do not force a line break or change equation structure if it would make the mathematics less readable or alter its meaning.

Apply the checklist as editorial guidance: do not introduce awkward wording merely to satisfy a mechanical rule. Where a rule appears to conflict with mathematical correctness or a user's explicit instruction, preserve correctness and the user's instruction, and call out the conflict when it affects the result.
